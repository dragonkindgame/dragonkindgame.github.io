I. CHOOSE A HUNTER

Four heroes, two combat distances

The official class split is clear: Knight and Barbarian fight up close; Oracle and Archer pressure enemies from range. Your preferred distance is the quickest first decision.

II. BUILD THE GRID

How an Ancestral Grid build comes together

  1. Find

    Loot active skills

    Skills are part of the loot chase, so the build begins with what you earn in combat rather than a fixed level-up tree.

  2. Place

    Fit skills into the grid

    Each hero has a distinct Ancestral Grid. Placement determines which nearby modifiers can influence a skill.

  3. Link

    Connect useful modifiers

    Prioritize modifiers that support the same damage, defense or utility plan instead of spreading power across unrelated effects.

  4. Test

    Align gear and Wyrmling

    Recheck the full loop—skills, modifiers, equipment and companion—when the build feels strong on paper but weak in combat.

The practical rule

Build around one outcome. A connected set of effects usually produces a clearer combat identity than individually attractive pieces that do not reinforce each other.

III. GROW THE EXPEDITION

The systems that progress beside your hero

Wyrmling
Your dragon companion evolves alongside the character and forms part of the build rather than serving only as decoration.
Ancestral Grid
A hero-specific board where looted skills and modifiers are arranged to create combinations and define playstyle.
City of Montescail
The central refuge grows as you progress. Building priorities, new residents and unlocked services expand what the city can do.
Co-op campaign
The adventure can be played online with up to four players, from arrival in Montescail through endgame activities.

FIELD QUESTIONS

Before you choose a build

Which Dragonkin class should I start with?

Start with combat distance. Pick Knight or Barbarian for close combat, or Oracle or Archer when you prefer ranged positioning. The better first hero is the one whose distance keeps you engaged with the encounter.

Is the Ancestral Grid a normal skill tree?

No. You acquire skills as loot and arrange them with modifiers on a hero-specific grid, so placement and combination matter alongside the skill itself.

Can the whole game be played in co-op?

The official Steam description states that the adventure supports online co-op for up to four players from Montescail through endgame activities.
